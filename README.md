# Privacy Policy for Santal

_Last updated: May 6, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how **iShowroom s.r.o.** ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the **Santal** mobile application.

## Overview

Santal is an application that includes augmented reality (AR) features. The app uses your device camera to support AR experiences, including scanning AR points and helping create floor plans inside the app.

## Information We Access

### Camera

Santal requires access to your device camera because the camera is a core part of the app's AR functionality.

The camera is used to:

- display and support augmented reality features
- scan AR points in the environment
- help create floor plans within the application

We do **not** use the camera for advertising purposes.

## How We Use Information

We use camera access only to provide the app's functionality described above.

## Data Storage and Sharing

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use camera access to collect information for marketing.
- Any data created by the app is used only for the operation of the app and its features.

If future versions of the app add features involving account data, cloud storage, analytics, or data sharing, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Permissions

Santal may request the following permission:

- `android.permission.CAMERA` - required for augmented reality features, scanning AR points, and creating floor plans

If you deny camera permission, some or all AR-related features may not work correctly.

## Children's Privacy

Santal is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
